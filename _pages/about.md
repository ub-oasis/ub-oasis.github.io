### Biography

I am an Associate Professor in the [Department of Computer Science and Engineering](https://engineering.buffalo.edu/computer-science-engineering.html) in the [School of Engineering and Applied Sciences (SEAS)](http://www.seas.buffalo.edu) at the [State University of New York (SUNY) at Buffalo](http://www.buffalo.edu/). I earned my Ph.D. in Computer Science from the [University of Minnesota](http://www.cs.umn.edu).

My research spans the broad area of <b>AI for Science</b> with a focus on developing scalable AI solutions that integrate algorithmic and infrastructure research for scientific discovery.

From 2021 to 2025, I served as a **Program Director** in the [Office of Advanced Cyberinfrastructure (OAC)](https://www.nsf.gov/div/index.jsp?div=OAC) at the [National Science Foundation (NSF)](https://www.nsf.gov).
