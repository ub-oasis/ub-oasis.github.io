## Teaching

Courses taught by Dr. Varun Chandola at the University at Buffalo:

<div class="section-card">
<div class="research-grid" style="grid-template-columns: 1fr;">

<div class="research-card" style="padding: 1.25rem;">
<div class="research-body">
<h4 class="research-title" style="font-size: 1.15rem;">CSE 474/574: Introduction to Machine Learning</h4>
<p class="research-desc">Comprehensive introduction to fundamental concepts and algorithms in machine learning, covering supervised learning (regression, classification, SVMs, neural networks), unsupervised learning (clustering, dimensionality reduction, EM), and learning theory.</p>
</div>
</div>

<div class="research-card" style="padding: 1.25rem;">
<div class="research-body">
<h4 class="research-title" style="font-size: 1.15rem;">CSE 601: Data Mining and Bioinformatics</h4>
<p class="research-desc">Advanced techniques for extracting actionable insights and patterns from massive biological, clinical, and complex structured datasets, including clustering, association rules, anomaly detection, and sequence mining.</p>
</div>
</div>

<div class="research-card" style="padding: 1.25rem;">
<div class="research-body">
<h4 class="research-title" style="font-size: 1.15rem;">Graduate Seminars in Scalable Data Science &amp; Anomaly Detection</h4>
<p class="research-desc">Research seminars exploring recent advances in non-linear manifold learning, deep generative models for graphs, and spatiotemporal anomaly detection.</p>
</div>
